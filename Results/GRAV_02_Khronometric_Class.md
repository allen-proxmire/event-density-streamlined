# ED's gravity is khronometric

**The claim.** ED's gravity is not quite general relativity. It carries Einstein's two gravitational-wave modes plus one extra scalar, because the arrow of time is written into the law itself and picks out a preferred slicing of time.

**Strength:** follows from the foundation, as a structural count rather than a full computation.

## How it goes

Because the arrow sits in the law, the symmetry available to the theory is not the full freedom to relabel spacetime, but only the freedom that preserves the time slicing. A smaller symmetry leaves more physical modes. Counting gives two tensor modes plus one scalar, which is the khronometric class.

## What this leans on, stated plainly

- The paper calls this structural, and says so plainly: it is an argument from the symmetry group, not a completed linearized calculation of the modes.
- It is an infrared statement only. ED does not inherit the ultraviolet behaviour of other theories in this class.
- The extra scalar's parameters are not computed in this paper.

## How it could be killed

A linearization of the dynamical rule that yields a different mode count, an extra vector mode for instance, would break the identification. So would the definite absence of any scalar gravitational-wave polarization at a sensitivity that excludes this mode, since the class requires one.

## What it is not

Not a claim that ED is general relativity, and not a claim that it is a specific published alternative. It is a statement about which class the theory lands in, and why.
