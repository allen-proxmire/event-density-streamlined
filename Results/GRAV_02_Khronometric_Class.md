# ED's gravitational class

**The claim.** The paper claims ED's gravity is khronometric: Einstein's two gravitational-wave modes plus one extra scalar, because the arrow of time written into the law picks out a preferred slicing of time.

**Strength:** Not established. The class is assumed, not derived. The only dynamics ED actually builds is a scalar diffusion equation, which has no waves and no tensor modes.
**Sector:** gravity. **Paper:** [GR-II_ED Gravity is Khronometric Class](../Papers/GR-II_ED%20Gravity%20is%20Khronometric%20Class.md). **Ledger row:** 67.

---

## What review found

- **The count "2 tensor + 1 scalar" is borrowed, not derived.** It is what khronometric gravity has. ED arrives at it by assuming it is a metric theory with a preferred foliation, then quoting the known count for that class.
- **ED's only built dynamics is a single scalar.** GR-III's rule, ḃ = D∇²b − κρ, is first-order in time and describes one scalar. A disturbance e^{i(kx−ωt)} gives ω = −iDk²: it decays without travelling, so the rule has no waves at all. No tensor sector is ever constructed from it.
- **Lovelock's theorem has nothing to apply to.** It needs field equations built from a metric, and ED has none.
- **What survives is a conjecture worth stating as one:** that a fundamental arrow of time implies a preferred foliation.

## How it goes

Because the arrow sits in the law, the argument goes, the available symmetry preserves the time slicing rather than allowing every relabeling of spacetime. Theories with that smaller symmetry are known to carry two tensor modes and one scalar.

## What this leans on, stated plainly

- The assumption that ED is a metric theory in this class. It is not shown from ED's dynamics.
- The extra scalar's parameters are not computed.

## How it could be killed

Constructing ED's dynamics for the metric and finding a different mode count. Empirically, excluding any scalar gravitational-wave polarization at the relevant sensitivity.

## What it is not

Not a derivation of ED's gravitational class. It is a conjecture about which class ED would fall into.
