# The Hawking temperature

**The claim.** The paper claims that near a horizon, the substrate's own cross-chain correlations become periodic in imaginary time, giving the temperature T = κ/2π.

**Strength:** Identification, not a derivation. T = κ/2π is the standard result, with κ borrowed. That ED's correlators carry the periodicity is asserted.
**Sector:** black holes. **Paper:** [Hawking Spectrum](../Papers/Hawking%20Spectrum.md). **Ledger row:** 36.

---

## What review found

- **Every input to the temperature is inherited:** κ, the horizon's location, and the Euclidean periodicity. The paper's own audit marks its §3 steps as inherited.
- **That V5 correlators are periodic is asserted by appeal to coarse-graining (DCGT)**, not computed.
- **The paper's separate "headline" prediction has no fixed content.** The prediction is a non-thermal correction of order (ω/ω_c)². Its cutoff is chosen (ω_c = c/ℓ_P), its parity is postulated (P-V5-Even) and its coefficient is undetermined. For any black hole well above the Planck mass it is unobservably small.
- **Analogue experiments cannot test that prediction.** An analogue horizon's deviations from thermality come from its medium's dispersion (Unruh 1995 onward), not from a Planck-scale substrate. The analogue falsifier on the earlier version of this page has been removed.

## How it goes

The paper asserts that the V5 correlators near the surface are periodic in imaginary time with period 2π/κ. A system periodic in imaginary time with period β is thermal at T = 1/β, so T = κ/2π and the spectrum is Planck-shaped.

## What this leans on, stated plainly

- The formula, κ and the periodicity all come from standard semiclassical physics. ED's addition is the claim that its correlators are what carry the periodicity.
- The non-thermal correction rests on an extra postulate, P-V5-Even, that is not on the foundation page. The temperature result does not need it.

## How it could be killed

There is no distinctive test. A substrate calculation showing the V5 correlators are not periodic would remove the identification. The temperature itself stands or falls with standard semiclassical physics.

## What it is not

Not a derivation of the Hawking formula, and not a testable prediction beyond it.
