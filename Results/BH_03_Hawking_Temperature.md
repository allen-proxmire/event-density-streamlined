## How it goes

The substrate's V5 correlators near the surface acquire periodicity in imaginary time. A system periodic in imaginary time with period β is thermal at T = 1/β. The period is 2π/κ, so T = κ/2π, and the spectrum is Planck-shaped.

## What this leans on, stated plainly

- ED's contribution here is that the *correlators* carry the periodicity, rather than matter fields on a fixed background. The 2π itself comes through the standard bridge.
- The paper's separate prediction of a non-thermal correction of size (ω/ω_c)² rests on an extra assumption, P-V5-Even, that the kernel's envelope is symmetric in frequency. That assumption is **not** on the foundation page. It belongs to this paper only, and the temperature result does not need it.

## How it could be killed

A substrate-level analysis showing the V5 correlators do not become periodic in imaginary time near the surface would take the temperature with it. The separate correction prediction dies if analogue experiments find a linear rather than quadratic deviation from thermal, or an enhancement rather than a suppression.

## What it is not

Not a standalone derivation of the Hawking formula. The formula is standard; what ED supplies is a substrate object that satisfies it.
