## How it goes

The bandwidth current is typed as a transported flux, so it is covariantly conserved. Build the rank-2 source from it for dust. Then the statement that its divergence vanishes is algebraically the statement that the dust moves on geodesics. For the null sector the same identity comes from the substrate's own shortest-path rule.

## What this leans on, stated plainly

- Exact for dust. Pressure and anisotropic stress need the full dynamical rule, which this paper does not supply.
- The metric it runs on comes from GR-I, whose lapse rests on a premise GR-III closes only modulo a band-accounting argument. Every gravity result here shares that exposure.

## How it could be killed

A demonstration that the substrate's bandwidth current is not the conserved flux this identification requires, or that its divergence for dust does not reduce to the geodesic condition, would remove the result. Empirically, any violation of the equivalence principle at leading order for pressureless matter.

## What it is not

Not a derivation of the full Einstein equations, and not a statement about matter with pressure.
