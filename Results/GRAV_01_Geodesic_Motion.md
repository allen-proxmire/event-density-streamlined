# Geodesic motion

**The claim.** The paper claims that in ED, "matter is conserved" and "matter falls along the straightest available path" are the same statement.

**Strength:** Textbook result, restated in ED terms. For dust, covariant conservation and geodesic motion are equivalent in any metric theory. ED's own input, that its bandwidth current is that conserved current, is asserted.
**Sector:** gravity. **Paper:** [GR-II_ED Gravity is Khronometric Class](../Papers/GR-II_ED%20Gravity%20is%20Khronometric%20Class.md). **Ledger row:** 66.

## How it goes

The bandwidth current is taken to be covariantly conserved. Build the dust source from it. The vanishing of that source's divergence is then algebraically the statement that the dust moves on geodesics.

## What this leans on, stated plainly

- Exact for dust. Pressure and stress need dynamics that ED does not supply.
- The conservation of the bandwidth current, which is asserted.

## How it could be killed

Empirically, a leading-order violation of the equivalence principle for pressureless matter, which would also contradict general relativity. For ED specifically: showing the bandwidth current is not covariantly conserved.

## What it is not

Not a derivation of the Einstein equations, and not a new result. It is the standard identity, stated for ED's current.
