## How they group

Ordered above from least to most dependent.

- **Three stand on the primitives and standard mathematics alone:** no anyons, charge as winding, parity is spontaneous.
- **Three add the two kernels:** the horizon mechanism, the Hawking temperature, the gauge groups.
- **Six sit on the gravity chain** and share one exposure: geodesic motion, the khronometric class, gravity waves at c, the khronon speed, α₂ = 0, and the entropy coefficient. All six pass through the emergent metric, whose lapse rests on a band-accounting premise that is argued rather than closed. **If that premise fails, all six fall together.**
- **Three run on the emergent acoustic metric,** so they carry the five working conditions: the kinetic-energy factor, the spinor frame, the one horizon law.
- **One rests on unaudited upstream work:** the primordial tensor modes. It should be quoted with that caveat until the three papers beneath it are read.

## What is deliberately not here
