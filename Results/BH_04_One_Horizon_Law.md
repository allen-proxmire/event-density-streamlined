## How it goes

For each of the four, ask what happens to cross-chain content at the surface. At a black hole it collapses; for an accelerated observer, content beyond the Rindler surface is cut off; beyond the cosmic horizon, regions are disconnected; in a fluid analogue, phonon-class content saturates the same way. One mechanism, four projections. The shared temperature law then follows from the shared mechanism.

## How it could be killed

A horizon-class object that does not show the universal thermal spectrum. Or substrate evidence that cross-chain saturation does not occur at one of the four. A fifth horizon class would extend the picture rather than break it.

## What it is not

Not a derivation of the Hawking-Unruh formula, and not a prediction of any of the four temperatures.
